## Western O-RAN Dense Mobile Challenge (ONE WORD)

**Role:** Co-Investigator  
**Funder:** Department for Science, Innovation and Technology (DSIT)  
**Project period:** 2023–2025

ONE WORD investigated machine-learning-based cell-load prediction and intelligent traffic steering in dense Open RAN networks.

Working with academic and industry partners, our traffic-steering algorithm was implemented as an O-RAN xApp and integrated with industry-developed control and radio-access components. Real-time testing on O-RAN infrastructure demonstrated cell barring and traffic steering, moving the research from algorithm development towards practical system integration.

### Research themes

- Open RAN (O-RAN)
- Network intelligence
- Machine learning for wireless networks
- Traffic steering and cell barring
- Industry integration and experimental validation
