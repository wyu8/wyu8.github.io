My research focuses on **open, intelligent and sustainable wireless communication systems**. I am particularly interested in developing intelligent networking and resource-management solutions, and in exploring how these solutions can be evaluated and applied in practical wireless systems through experimental validation and collaboration with academic and industry partners.

## Open & Intelligent RAN

Open Radio Access Networks (O-RAN) provide new opportunities to introduce intelligence, flexibility and programmability into traditionally closed mobile-network architectures.

My research in this area explores **network intelligence, cell-load prediction, traffic steering and resource management**, with a particular interest in machine-learning-enabled control through the RAN Intelligent Controller. An important part of this work is moving intelligent networking algorithms beyond simulation and towards implementation and validation in practical O-RAN systems.

**Related project:** [ONE WORD]({% link _projects/oneword.md %})

## Connected Mobility

Future connected and autonomous mobility depends on reliable, low-latency and adaptive communication across increasingly heterogeneous wireless environments.

My research investigates **vehicle-to-everything (V2X) communications, multi-RAT connectivity, sensing-assisted communications and intelligent traffic steering**. I am particularly interested in how network sensing and environmental information can support proactive connectivity decisions and improve communication reliability for connected vehicles.

**Related project:** [TRACE-V2X]({% link _projects/trace-v2x.md %})

## Sustainable 6G

The growing scale and complexity of future wireless and computing infrastructures make sustainability an increasingly important design consideration.

My research explores **energy-efficient and carbon-aware resource allocation** across communication and edge–fog–cloud systems. This includes the use of reinforcement learning and other AI techniques to adapt computing, communication and power resources to changing demand while reducing energy use and environmental impact.

**Related project:** [SustainAIRA6G]({% link _projects/sustainaira6g.md %})

## Research Collaboration

My research is developed through collaborations with academic and industry partners in the UK and internationally. These collaborations span wireless communications, networking, AI and distributed computing, and include joint research projects, publications, research visits and experimental work using 5G and V2X facilities.

I welcome enquiries from prospective **PhD students, visiting researchers and collaborators** interested in O-RAN, V2X communications, intelligent wireless networks and sustainable 6G systems.
