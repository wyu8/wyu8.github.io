I am a Lecturer in the School of Computing and Communications at Lancaster University. My research focuses on open, intelligent and sustainable wireless communication systems, spanning O-RAN, V2X communications, and energy-efficient 6G networks.

I develop intelligent resource-management and networking solutions and work with academic and industry partners to translate these methods into practical wireless systems, bridging theoretical research with real-world implementation and experimental validation.
