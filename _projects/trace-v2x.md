## Smart and Proactive Multi-RAT Traffic Steering for V2X

**Role:** Principal Investigator  
**Funder:** Horizon Europe Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions (MSCA), supported through the UKRI Horizon Europe Guarantee  
**Project period:** 2024–2027

TRACE-V2X develops intelligent and proactive traffic-steering solutions for vehicle-to-everything (V2X) communications. The project explores how sensing, cooperative environmental perception and multi-RAT connectivity can be combined to support reliable and low-latency communications for connected and autonomous vehicles.

### Research themes

- V2X communications
- Multi-RAT and multi-connectivity
- Intelligent traffic steering
- Sensing-assisted communications
- 5G/6G experimental validation
