## SustainAIRA6G

**Role:** Co-Investigator  
**Funder:** EPSRC / CHEDDAR  
**Project period:** 2024–2025

SustainAIRA6G investigated AI-driven resource allocation for sustainable 6G edge–fog–cloud systems.

The research explored reinforcement-learning-based approaches to managing computing and communication resources under changing demand, using energy-consumption and carbon-emissions information to improve the sustainability of future wireless systems.

The project brought together researchers from Lancaster University, the University of Exeter, Durham University and the University of Warwick, combining expertise in wireless communications, AI and distributed computing to explore more sustainable approaches to future 6G systems.

### Research themes

- Sustainable 6G
- AI-driven resource allocation
- Edge–fog–cloud computing
- Energy efficiency and carbon reduction
- Reinforcement learning
