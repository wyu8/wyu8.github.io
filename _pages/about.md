I am a Lecturer in the School of Computing and Communications at Lancaster University. My research focuses on open, intelligent and sustainable wireless communication systems, with particular interests in O-RAN, V2X communications and energy-efficient 6G resource management.

My work spans both theoretical development and practical system integration, with a growing focus on translating intelligent networking algorithms into real-world wireless systems through collaboration with academic and industry partners.
